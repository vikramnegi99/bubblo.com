Policies

Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to process and deliver your order — your name, mobile number and delivery address, and your email if you choose to share it. We use it to fulfil your order and to support you if you contact us. We do not sell your data.

Terms & Conditions

By placing an order you confirm the details you provide are accurate and that you can receive delivery at the given address. Prices, offers and availability are shown at checkout and may change. Every COD order is confirmed by a call from our team before dispatch.

Shipping Policy

Orders are dispatched to serviceable pincodes across India. Delivery timelines depend on your location and the shipping provider; a specific timeline is shown only where the store has configured one. Shipping charges, if any, appear at checkout before you order.

Return / Replacement Policy

If your product arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us with your Order ID and a photo. Any return or replacement window is offered only when enabled by the store and shown here — we do not promise a return or replacement unless it is configured.

Contact

For any policy question, reach us via the Contact page. These policies can be edited by the store owner at any time.
